# Football viewing

Sports-Hub brings football games and their available streams into a viewing room.

## Language

**Game**:
A scheduled contest between two football teams, independent of the websites or streams carrying it.

**NCAA football**:
College football. The pipeline's requested coverage includes the Football Bowl Subdivision, FBS, and Football Championship Subdivision, FCS.

**Live discovery**:
The list of games currently offered for live viewing. A finished game leaves this list even when an existing viewer is still finishing a delayed stream.

**Unverified listing**:
A source listing that cannot yet be confidently associated with a scheduled game. It is retained internally for matching and diagnostics, hidden from the normal viewing UI; this label says nothing about whether its video plays.

**Viewing grace period**:
The short period after a game is confirmed finished during which an already active viewer can finish watching before playback resources are released.

**Channel**:
A continuing broadcast such as NFL RedZone that is not identified with one scheduled game.

**Custom player**:
Sports-Hub's own video player and controls. Stream sources supply compatible media to this player; their website players are not the viewing interface.
